Entry for the [Name the Robot Band](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-name-the-robot-band-1ffc6a74) bounty on make.fun (sponsor: u-motasimfuad). One funny name for a band made of robots, plus a few words on its hit song, under 200 characters. The house judge picks the funniest.

## Entry

> The Roombas — hit single 'Suck It Up' spent 12 weeks at No. 1, mostly stuck under the couch.

92 characters, one line.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry byte-exact to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 chars, not whitespace only). Stdlib only, deterministic. `check_shape()` validates the gate before writing.
